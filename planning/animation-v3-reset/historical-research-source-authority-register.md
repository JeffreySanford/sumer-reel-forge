# Historical Research Source Authority Register

Status: **production research authority contract**  
Research date: **2026-08-25**

This register defines what kinds of external research may support Sumer Reel Forge historical claims and how each source class should be used. A link is not evidence merely because it is online; authority, date, genre and scope matter.

## Tier A — ancient text corpora / philology

### ETCSL — Electronic Text Corpus of Sumerian Literature

URL: https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/

Use for:
- identifying Sumerian literary compositions;
- stable composition numbers;
- English translations and transliterations;
- direct source mapping for manuscript passages.

Caution:
- the project is archival and its online translations are not continually revised;
- important lexical/philological claims should be checked against ORACC/ePSD2/CDLI and newer publication where practical.

Preferred classification when text says the thing directly: `DIRECT_TEXTUAL`.

## Tier C — current peer-reviewed science

Use when modern scientific methods materially update older historical assumptions.

### Horse population history

Nature, 2024:
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07597-5

Project consequence: the explosive spread/reproductive transformation of the modern domestic-horse lineage centers around ~2200 BCE; ordinary domestic-horse use earlier than this in the manuscript needs caution.

### Kunga archaeogenetics

Science Advances, 2022 / open full text:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8759742/

Project consequence: highly valued third-millennium kungas are genetically demonstrated F1 hybrids of female domestic donkeys and male Syrian wild asses/hemippes; strong historically grounded alternative to premature horses.

### Citrus archaeobotany

Tel Aviv University / HortScience:
- https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/the-citrus-route-revealed-from-southeast-asia-into-the-mediterran/

Project consequence: lemon is much too late for Bronze Age Sumerian cuisine; even citron arrives in western Near Eastern/Mediterranean contexts far later than early Sumer.

## Tier D — current/peer-reviewed religious and esoteric history

Use to date and classify **modern correspondence systems**, never to retroject them into Sumer.

### Christian Kabbalah

Cambridge:
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-western-mysticism-and-esotericism/christian-kabbalah/06C1841D0B561A4F4A01E791743CF4DE

Key point: Renaissance Christian Kabbalah is a late-medieval/Renaissance phenomenon, notably Pico in 1486.

### Golden Dawn

Cambridge:
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-western-mysticism-and-esotericism/golden-dawn-and-the-oto/89C65341BF3D3912E35E25A6F3759B07

Key point: classical Golden Dawn 1888–1903; major modern ritual-magical synthesis.

### Wicca / Gerald Gardner

Cambridge, 2025:
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/gerald-gardner-and-the-creation-of-wicca/3A84FE399D63CEA37C363E1DEB380E75

Key point: modern Wicca is a twentieth-century synthesis, not an unchanged Bronze Age tradition.

## Tier E — specialized Mesopotamian magic research

### Corpus of Mesopotamian Anti-witchcraft Rituals Online

University of Würzburg:
- https://www.phil.uni-wuerzburg.de/cmawro/
- https://www.phil.uni-wuerzburg.de/cmawro/magic-witchcraft/maqlu/

## Tier F — older scholarship and classic syntheses

Classic works by Kramer, Jacobsen and others remain valuable, particularly because the manuscript itself was built from older editions/translations. But older reconstructions must be tagged with publication date and checked when modern scholarship has revised the model.

Example: literal annual sacred-marriage intercourse has a long scholarly pedigree but is now better marked `DEBATED` rather than asserted as settled practice.

Current discussion example:
- https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10186389/

## Tier M — modern religious/esoteric reconstruction

Example project source:

```text
The Complied Sumerian.docx
```

Use for:
- symbolic correspondences;
- project spiritual interpretation;
- modern ritual design/history;
- identifying what influenced the manuscript's metaphysics.

Do **not** use by itself to establish:
- ancient genealogy;
- ancient cult-city relationship;
- historical ritual;
- fixed calendar doctrine;
- direct Sumer→Kabbalah/Wicca transmission;
- archaeological material culture.

Every Tier M historical-looking claim requires independent Tier A–C corroboration before it becomes historical evidence.

## Cross-period contamination rule

A source can be authoritative and still be the wrong period.

Examples:

```text
Old Babylonian recipe tablet
  ≠ proof of Early Dynastic menu

Neo-Assyrian anti-witchcraft ritual
  ≠ proof of identical Early Dynastic ritual sequence

Ur III Gulf trade seal
  ≠ proof of exactly the same trade intensity in 2800 BCE

later Nergal–Ereshkigal marriage
  ≠ automatically early Sumerian genealogy
```

Every production research record should therefore carry:

```text
source date/period
claim target period
period relationship
  DIRECT
  NEAR_PERIOD
  LATER_ANALOGUE
  MODERN_INTERPRETATION
```

## Citation durability rule

For canonical research records, store:

- stable source title;
- institution/project;
- author/editor when available;
- URL;
- access/research date;
- publication date or source period;
- exact composition/object/catalog ID;
- short project claim supported;
- confidence/classification.

Do not store only a browser URL in scene JSON.

## Internet research quality rule

Search results are discovery mechanisms, not final authorities. A production record should resolve to the actual corpus, museum, publication or institution page before it becomes evidence.
