# Chapter 1 - Enki

## Summary

Chapter 1 follows Enki through the voyage toward Dilmun, the meeting with local peoples, the founding and watering of Dilmun, the planning of canals, the storm near Kutu, the birth of city/goddess identities, the garden and plant cycle, the E-Absu, and An's reflective creation account.
